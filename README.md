# 📒 Geometry Dash Book

**Your personal progress journal for Geometry Dash.**
Pick the demon you dream of beating, and the journal builds a path of levels to get you there, one stage at a time.

[![Open the journal](https://img.shields.io/badge/▶_Open_the_journal-ff2d87?style=for-the-badge)](https://zmykhalskiy-alt.github.io/GD-Book/)
[![Download for Windows](https://img.shields.io/badge/⬇_Windows_app-7b2cff?style=for-the-badge)](https://github.com/zmykhalskiy-alt/GD-Book/releases/latest)

## ✨ What it does

- 🎯 **Goal and path**: choose your goal demon (Bloodbath, Acropolis, LIMBO and more) and get a step-by-step path from easier levels up to it
- 📝 **Level log**: record attempts, your best percent and beaten levels for hundreds of official levels and demons
- 📊 **Difficulty scale**: see what comes after what you've already beaten
- 🛠️ **Creator course**: lessons on building your own levels in the editor, from the basics to triggers and design, with quizzes
- 🏆 **Trophies and XP**: level up, keep day streaks and unlock achievements
- 👤 **Accounts**: sign up with your nick, log in, keep several players on one device
- 🧭 **Tutorial**: a guided tour after sign-up, and a TUTORIAL button any time
- 🌍 **12 languages**: English, Українська, Polski, Deutsch, Español, Français, Português, Italiano, Türkçe, 日本語, 한국어, 中文

## 📱 On your phone

Open **https://zmykhalskiy-alt.github.io/GD-Book/** in Safari or Chrome →
**Share → Add to Home Screen**. It opens full screen, like an app.

> ⚠️ Don't download the `.html` file on a phone: a phone's file preview can't run it. Use the link.

## 💻 On Windows

Download **GeometryDashBook.exe** from [Releases](https://github.com/zmykhalskiy-alt/GD-Book/releases/latest) and run it.
If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

## 🔒 Your data

Progress is saved on your device only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
